🎨 1. Public Domain Status of the Original Paintings

The Impressionist paintings depicted (by Claude Monet, Camille Pissarro, Pierre-Auguste Renoir, Alfred Sisley, Eugène Boudin, Paul Cézanne, Edgar Degas, and contemporaries) were created between the 1860s and 1920s and are in the worldwide Public Domain as original two-dimensional works. That status covers the paintings themselves - it does not automatically extend to a specific video production about them. A faithful, unedited photographic reproduction of a public-domain painting does not gain new copyright protection (Bridgeman Art Library v. Corel Corp., 36 F. Supp. 2d 191). Most titles in this index are not that: the majority are AI-animated reimaginings, edited compilations, or otherwise transformed video productions created by third-party YouTube channels, and that production work is a separate, potentially copyrightable creative contribution belonging to its creator.

📺 2. YouTube Video Presentation & Direct Attribution

L'Impressionnisme Vivant does not host, re-encode, or redistribute full video files. All video playback is served through standard YouTube privacy-enhanced embedded players (youtube-nocookie.com). Video playback directly supports the originating creators—including LearnFromMasters, Muse Visual Art, Beautiful Living Art, and other channels—by routing official view counts, watch time, and advertising revenue directly to their YouTube accounts. Direct channel links and YouTube watch links are prominently attributed on every title card and modal.

🖼️ 3. Wallpaper Snapshots - Downloads Are Restricted by Default

Wallpaper snapshots are frames extracted directly from the videos described in Section 1. Because most videos here are third-party creative productions rather than faithful photo reproductions, download access defaults to view-only for every channel until its terms have been individually reviewed and it's been explicitly allowlisted as safe to redistribute stills from. A channel showing "Free Wallpaper Download" has gone through that review; everything else remains fullscreen/slideshow-viewable but not downloadable, regardless of how old or well-known the depicted painting is. If you are a content creator and believe your channel's classification is wrong in either direction, contact the curator (Section 5).

🎵 4. Classical Audio Suite

The ambient classical audio suite (Debussy, Satie, Ravel) is currently disabled. The musical compositions are public domain, but the specific recordings bundled with this project have unverified provenance - a sound recording carries its own copyright independent of the composition, and these files carry no metadata identifying a performer, source, or license. The feature will be re-enabled only once each recording's provenance and license are confirmed, or the files are replaced with verifiably-licensed recordings. See audio/README.md for details.

📬 5. Rights Holder Contact

This is a small, individually-curated project, not a platform hosting user-submitted content, so no statutory safe-harbor process applies here - this is a direct notice-and-response contact, not a DMCA agent designation. If you are a copyright holder or YouTube channel creator and have questions, concerns, or removal/reclassification requests regarding any title or frame snapshot in this index, please contact curator sachin g directly at lgtkgtv@gmail.com.
