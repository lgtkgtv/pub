Rights & Reproduction Doctrine

Under Bridgeman Art Library v. Corel Corp. (1999) and EU CDSM Directive Art. 14, faithful 2D digital photographic reproductions of public-domain paintings lack independent creative originality and cannot receive new copyright protection.

100% Verified Public Domain (1470–1944)

All 10 historic masters featured in this exhibition passed away between 1510 and 1944. Every masterwork is in the undisputed worldwide public domain under international Life + 70 rules. Active 20th-century estate works have been deliberately excluded.
