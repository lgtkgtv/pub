## 🎨 Key Features

1. **👑 The Crown Jewels Master Gallery**: 60 iconic paintings displayed in rich detail with physical dimensions and resolution metrics.
2. **🎧 The 5-Minute Guided Tour**: A 10-milestone chronological journey through art history (1485 Early Renaissance to 1908 Vienna Secession) from Botticelli to Klimt.
3. **🖼️ "Real-Life Size" Museum Wall Visualizer**: Solves screen scale distortion by comparing paintings against an accurate 175 cm (5'9") human silhouette.
4. **🔬 Curator's 3.0× Detail Loupe**: Circular high-magnification overlay on desktop and touch-offset mobile inspection.

## 📜 Legal & Rights Summary

* **100% Worldwide Public Domain (1470–1944)**: Every featured artwork was created by masters deceased prior to 1945 (Life + 70 and US pre-1929 expired).
* **Proactive Estate Exclusion**: Modern 20th-century artists under active copyright administration have been deliberately excluded to ensure absolute copyright safety.
* **Non-Commercial Educational Intent**: This project has zero business interest, monetization, or commercial print reproduction.
* **Zero-Dispute Takedown**: If any museum or rights holder requests an update or removal, please email `lgtkgtv@gmail.com` and it will be fulfilled within 24 hours.
