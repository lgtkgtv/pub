# 📜 Pantheon Fine Art Archive: Notice & Takedown Policy

**Effective Date**: September 2026  
**Curator Contact**: [`lgtkgtv@gmail.com`](mailto:lgtkgtv@gmail.com)  
**Project Classification**: Personal, Non-Commercial Educational & Art Appreciation Project  

---

## 1. Our Commitment to Safety and Respect

Pantheon is a personal, non-commercial educational project created out of deep appreciation for art history and the preservation of cultural heritage. There is **no commercial activity, advertising, sponsorship, print sales, or monetary transaction** associated with this repository or web application.

All visual artworks curated in this project were created between 1470 and 1918 by historic masters who passed away between 1510 and 1944, placing their underlying works in the worldwide public domain.

However, we understand that copyright administrations, museum foundations, and digital archives maintain differing policies and rights interpretations. Our policy is simple: **we prioritize safety, mutual respect, and zero dispute**.

---

## 2. Zero-Friction Removal Process

If you are a copyright owner, authorized estate representative, museum rights administrator, or digital archive operator and you believe that any image link, catalog metadata entry, or reference on this site:
- Concerns an active copyright or estate administration,
- Conflicts with your institution’s terms or concession policies, or
- Is improperly attributed or described:

**We will immediately and permanently remove or update the referenced item within 24 hours upon receipt of your email.**

No formal legal notices, notarizations, or statutory summons are required. A simple, informal message will receive our immediate cooperation.

---

## 3. How to Submit a Request

Please email us directly at:
> 📧 **`lgtkgtv@gmail.com`**  
> **Subject**: `[Pantheon Notice] Artwork / Content Inquiry`

To help us act as swiftly as possible, please include:
1. The **title of the artwork** and **artist name**.
2. The specific **URL or file path** in the repository or web exhibition.
3. The desired action (e.g., *immediate removal*, *updated attribution statement*, or *replacement with an authorized open-access URL*).

---

## 4. Immediate Compliance

Upon receiving your request:
1. The referenced item will be immediately removed from the active catalog and web application.
2. The change will be committed and pushed to the public GitHub repository within **24 hours**.
3. A confirmation email will be sent back to you detailing the action taken.

We thank the cultural heritage community, museums, and digital archivists for their ongoing dedication to preserving human artistic expression.
